## SEA-LION v4.8 (Latest)

SEA-LION version 4.8, released in Sep 2026, is our latest collection of foundational, text generation optimized for Southeast Asia. Utilizing open dataset for continue pretraining and advanced post-training methodologies—such as agentic on-policy distillation delivers state-of-the-art regional fluency and high computational efficiency.
