## SEA-LION v5

SEA-LION version 5, released in November 2026, scales our Southeast Asian multimodal and agentic capabilities from resource-constrained edge devices to massive enterprise reasoning clusters. Built on Gemma-4, Qwen3.5/Qwen4Exp, and Nemotron 3.5 Bolt foundations—and aligned via On-Policy Distillation (OPD)—this multi-architecture suite leverages expansive tokenizers supporting up to 8,900 native non-Latin characters to deliver state-of-the-art regional fluency and computational efficiency.

### Qwen-SEA-LION-v5 (397B, 27B & 9B Series)

Our flagship causal and vision-language series anchored by a 397B Mixture of Experts (MoE) model adapted to the Qwen4Exp architecture. It pairs QSA Hybrid Attention and Gated Residuals with a CPU-offloaded 328 GB N-gram Embedding memory table, delivering frontier multimodal reasoning and long-context efficiency across enterprise and mid-tier deployments.

### Gemma-SEA-LION-v5 (E2B and E4B Series)

Our ultra-efficient edge series built on Gemma-4. Leveraging native support, it is optimized for low-latency multimodal processing, precise function-calling, and autonomous agentic tool-use on constrained devices. Nemotron 3.5 Bolt engineered for rapid inference speed and balanced reasoning upon Gemma4-E4B, it provides a compact, enterprise-ready footprint for regional text generation and structured workflows.
